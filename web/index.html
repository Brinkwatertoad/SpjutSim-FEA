            Results

            ▶ How to read these results

            The headline is the peak von Mises stress among unaveraged solver recovery samples throughout the solid. A sampled maximum is not an exact continuum maximum.

            The smoothed surface contour first averages recovery samples within each element, then takes an unweighted mean of adjacent element values at each node. Surface extrema include only nodes used by the displayed boundary. The von Mises color scale spans zero to the whole-model sample peak, so its top color may not appear on the surface. Smoothing can reduce peaks.

            Yield factor of safety uses the unaveraged sample peak and the smaller available tensile/compressive yield strength. One linear solve does not establish safety; review convergence, constraints and stress concentrations.
