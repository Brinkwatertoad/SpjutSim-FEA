# Third-party software

No third-party runtime binaries are included in the initial framework commit.

Planned runtime dependencies are:

- Three.js, pinned and vendored in a classic/global browser-consumable form.
- Gmsh + OpenCASCADE, pinned and vendored as a local-file-compatible single-threaded WebAssembly build.

Versions, licenses, source URLs, checksums, build flags, and local modifications must be recorded here before artifacts are committed. Distribution of Gmsh carries GPL-2.0-or-later obligations unless separately licensed; the project must settle its distribution posture before public v1.0 distribution.
